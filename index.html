Who we are

A trusted name for the journey that matters most.

Fly to Hijaz Travels & Tours Pvt Ltd is based in Abbottabad and approved by the Ministry of Religious Affairs (MoRA), Pakistan, to arrange Hajj and Umrah travel. We work exclusively on the ISB–JED–ISB route with Pakistan International Airlines, pairing every departure with a choice of Makkah and Madinah hotels at different distances from the Haram — so every family can choose what fits their comfort and budget.

From the moment you confirm your package, our team — led by Mufti Umair Waheed and Qari Umer — handles your visa, accommodation, transport, and ziyarat arrangements, so your only focus is the journey itself.
